Most festive traffic comes in cold. A shopper from a reel has no account and no history with you, so the first screens treat her like a stranger, and the delivery question waits until she types a pincode.

Without Fastrr

With Fastrr

Chapter 5 · Payment and risk
Thu 26 Nov · 9:10 pm

She pays in one tap. Behind the scenes, every order is read before it ships.

Most shoppers want to pay and move on. But a ₹7,000 kurta set sent on cash on delivery that is refused costs you two-way shipping and a garment that comes back. COD orders carry about 65% higher RTO risk, and the network can tell which ones are likely to return before you dispatch.

Without Fastrr (public pages today)
  • An extra 5% off on prepaid orders, a good nudge.
  • Cash on delivery is available, as your FAQ describes.
  • Every order is treated alike at dispatch.
With Fastrr
  • QuickPay and payment personalisation, so each shopper sees the method most likely to complete.
  • RTO risk tags before the order is packed.
  • Pay After Delivery for hesitant shoppers.

RTO risk tags and rules

Scored from pincode, address and buyer history against real delivery outcomes. You set the rule: show only prepaid or Pay After Delivery for a high-risk first-time COD order, or switch COD off for it.

Pay After Delivery

A hesitant shopper approves with UPI and is charged only after delivery. It cannot be cancelled in transit, and the courier sees a prepaid parcel.

Chapter 6 · After
Fri 27 Nov onwards

The date she saw is the date she gets, and a missed delivery is caught early.

After the order, Neha waits. The promise she saw at checkout is what she measures everything against, and a failed delivery the day before a sangeet is the worst outcome for her and for you.

Without Fastrr (public pages today)
  • Orders are dispatched within 24 hours, with tracking through Shiprocket.
  • Size exchanges within 14 days, in line with your policy.
With Fastrr
  • The same delivery date, tracked through the network.
  • Failed-delivery (NDR) tags, so a bad address or an absent buyer is flagged early.
  • One-tap reorder for the next occasion through SSO.

NDR tags

When a delivery attempt fails, the reason is tagged and acted on quickly, so the outfit is not sent back a day before the occasion.

Reorder with SSO

For the next wedding, Neha signs in with one tap and her address is already there.
